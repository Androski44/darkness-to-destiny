THE BOOK I NEEDED WHEN I WAS DROWNING

Nobody handed me a map when I was coming up. No father sitting me down explaining what a man is supposed to be. What I got instead was the streets, the noise, and everybody's broken definition of manhood -- be hard, don't feel, take what you can. I lived that. It took me into addiction, into betrayal cycles, into rooms I barely walked out of.

From Darkness to Destiny is the book I wish somebody had put in my hands back then. Not theory -- a companion. Each chapter builds on the last, and every one ends with action steps, because reading about your life changing doesn't change your life. Doing does.

"Religion won't save you -- only inner fire will. Nobody is coming to rescue you. But the fire is already in you."

WHO IT'S FOR

Young men, 16 to 35, who are tired of guessing. Tired of the noise telling them who to be. Read it slow. Do the action steps. Come back to it when life hits -- it's built to be revisited, not read once and shelved.
